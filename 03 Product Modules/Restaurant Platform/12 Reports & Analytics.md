# Purpose

The Reports & Analytics module provides operational, financial, and business intelligence reporting for restaurant administrators.

It transforms transactional business data into meaningful insights without becoming the system of record.

It provides:

- Sales Reports
- Order Reports
- Reservation Reports
- Customer Analytics
- Branch Performance
- Operational KPIs
- Trend Analysis
- Dashboard Metrics

Reports & Analytics serves as the centralized reporting engine for the Restaurant Platform.

---

# Scope

This specification defines:

- Reporting architecture
- Dashboard metrics
- Operational analytics
- Financial reports
- Customer analytics
- Reservation analytics
- Performance reporting
- Business intelligence

---

# Out of Scope

This specification does not define:

- Transaction processing
- Data modification
- Accounting
- Payment processing
- Machine learning implementation

These responsibilities belong to their respective modules.

# Engineering Rules

## Rule RA-001

Reports & Analytics shall operate as a read-only business intelligence module.

It shall never modify transactional business data.

---

## Rule RA-002

All reports shall be generated from authoritative platform modules.

Business data shall never be duplicated solely for reporting purposes unless explicitly required for performance optimization.

---

## Rule RA-003

Every report shall belong to exactly one restaurant tenant.

Tenant isolation shall be enforced for every reporting operation.

---

## Rule RA-004

Branch-specific reports shall only include data belonging to the selected branch.

Cross-branch aggregation shall require appropriate administrative permissions.

---

## Rule RA-005

Dashboard metrics shall be derived from completed business transactions and operational events.

Metrics shall never be manually edited.

---

## Rule RA-006

Historical reports shall remain immutable.

Subsequent business operations shall not modify previously generated historical reporting periods.

---

## Rule RA-007

Every report export shall generate an audit record.

---

## Rule RA-008

Reports & Analytics shall communicate with other platform modules exclusively through documented APIs, domain events, and shared platform services.

---

## Rule RA-009

Reports containing sensitive financial or customer information shall enforce platform authorization and privacy policies.

---

## Rule RA-010

This document is the authoritative Reports & Analytics specification for the FluxDine platform.
