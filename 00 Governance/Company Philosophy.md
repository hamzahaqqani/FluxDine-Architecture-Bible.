# Purpose

This document defines the organizational philosophy that guides FluxDine's decisions, culture, priorities, and growth.

---

# Customer First

The restaurant is the primary customer.

Business decisions should begin with:

> What problem does this solve for the restaurant?

Technology should serve customer outcomes.

---

# Ownership

FluxDine believes in ownership at every level.

The company should encourage ownership of:

- Customer outcomes
- Code
- Architecture
- Decisions
- Documentation
- Operational reliability

---

# Accountability

Ownership requires accountability.

Teams and individuals are expected to:

- Understand their responsibilities.
- Communicate risks.
- Surface problems early.
- Correct mistakes.
- Learn from failures.

---

# Transparency

Important decisions should be explainable.

FluxDine favors:

- Documented decisions
- Clear ownership
- Explicit responsibilities
- Traceable changes
- Honest communication

---

# Sustainable Growth

Growth should not destroy the foundation that enables growth.

The company shall prioritize:

- Sustainable engineering
- Customer retention
- Operational reliability
- Healthy economics
- Long-term product value

over vanity metrics.

---

# Innovation

Innovation is encouraged when it produces meaningful value.

Technology should not be adopted merely because it is new.

The evaluation should consider:

- Customer value
- Reliability
- Cost
- Maintainability
- Security
- Strategic fit

---

# Operational Excellence

A platform is only valuable if it operates reliably.

FluxDine therefore treats:

- Monitoring
- Logging
- Testing
- Security
- Backups
- Incident response

as core engineering responsibilities.

---

# Learning Culture

Failures should generate learning.

When something goes wrong:

```text
Incident
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Correct
   ↓
Document
   ↓
Improve
```

Blame is less valuable than understanding the system that allowed the failure.

---

# Documentation Culture

Important knowledge should not exist only inside people's heads.

Architecture, decisions, workflows, and standards should be documented.

---

# Engineering Culture

FluxDine values:

- Clarity
- Discipline
- Curiosity
- Responsibility
- Continuous improvement

---

# Decision Philosophy

When making decisions:

1. Understand the problem.
2. Identify constraints.
3. Consider alternatives.
4. Evaluate long-term consequences.
5. Make the decision explicit.
6. Document significant architectural decisions.

---

# Company Principle

> **Build deliberately. Operate responsibly. Improve continuously.**
